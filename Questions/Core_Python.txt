1. What is the difference between an iterator and a generator in Python?
    >>> numbers = (num for num in range(5))
    >>> print(type(numbers))

    Why the generator taken as memory efficient?

    Can you iterate over a generator twice?

        No, not normally. A generator is single-use. Once it is exhausted, you cannot iterate over
        it again.
