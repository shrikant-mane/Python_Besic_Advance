What is Encapsulation in Python?

Encapsulation is the OOP principle of bundling data and the methods that operate on that data
inside a class, while controlling how that internal state is accessed or modified

Encapsulation asks:
How do I control access to the object's internal state?

method()       → Public    → "Use me"
_method()      → Protected → "Internal; subclasses may use me"
__method()     → Private   → "Internal to this class"
